Our Core Services

GreenMetrics offers a wide range of services that help businesses adopt sustainable practices, reduce their environmental impact, and enhance their competitiveness in the emerging green economy through scientific innovation and advanced tools.

Module 01

1. Environmental Footprint Analysis

Helping organizations understand, measure, and reduce their overall environmental impact through scientifically grounded methodologies and tools.

a. Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)

Standardized method assessing environmental impacts across all stages of a product or service lifecycle — from raw material extraction to end-of-life disposal.

What it includes:
  • Mapping energy & material flows
  • Quantifying impacts (CO₂, water, resources)
  • Scenario analysis for eco-design
Tools used:

SimaPro, GaBi, OpenLCA

Use cases:

Eco-friendly product development, EPD & ISO 14040 support

b. Carbon Footprint Analysis

Quantifying greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions from business operations, products, or services, expressed accurately in CO₂-equivalents.

What it includes:
  • Identification of Scope 1, 2 & 3 emissions
  • Verified standards calculations
  • Reduction strategies & net-zero pathways
Tools used:

GHG Protocol, ISO 14064, Carbon Accounting Software

Use cases:

Corporate disclosures (CDP, ESG), carbon tax readiness

c. Water Footprint Assessment

Evaluating freshwater volumes used directly or indirectly, identifying vulnerabilities, consumption rates, and water pollution risks.

What it includes:
  • Quantification of blue, green & grey water
  • Spatial water stress analysis
  • Water conservation & mitigation plans
Tools used:

Water Footprint Network, ISO 14046, Aqueduct Atlas

Use cases:

Water-intensive industries (Agri, F&B, Textiles), standard compliance
